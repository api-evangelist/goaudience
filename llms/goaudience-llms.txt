# GoAudience

> GoAudience is a customer-intelligence and decision-layer product for Shopify ecommerce
> brands. It reads a merchant's first-party Shopify and Klaviyo data, builds stable
> customer personas from real purchase behavior, and returns ranked, actionable
> recommendations across acquisition, retention and margin. GoAudience is a
> Techstars-backed company.

## What it is

GoAudience sells an end-user application to Shopify merchants, not a developer platform.
It is installed as a Shopify app and used through a web console; there is no public API,
no SDK, no developer portal and no API reference.

Core capabilities described on the vendor's own site:

- Persona building — derives stable customer personas from real store purchase data
  rather than survey or third-party segments.
- Findings and recommended actions, ranked by revenue impact.
- Audience sync into downstream marketing tools: Klaviyo, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads.
- A natural-language "conversation" surface for asking grounded questions about the
  merchant's own customers. Plan quotas are metered in these conversations.
- Monday Brief — a weekly digest of at-risk, lapsed and recovered customers with revenue
  impact attached.
- Periodic brand and email audits.

## Pricing

Five tiers, banded by the merchant's annual revenue and metered in conversations per
month: Free ($0, sample persona only), Starter ($129/mo, ~1,500 conversations, brands
under $5M), Boost ($419/mo, ~4,000 conversations, $5M–$15M), Plus ($1,099/mo, unlimited
under fair use, $15M–$60M), and Enterprise (contact sales, $60M+, adds custom
integrations and data export). Annual billing gives two months free. No tier is priced
per API call, and no tier advertises API access.
